📜 Server Rules

💥 1. Be Yourself & Be Kind
• Everyone is welcome to be who they are here.
• No bullying, hate speech, or drama. Treat everyone with respect.
❤️ 2. Dating is Allowed (Keep it Respectful)
• Dating and flirting are fine, but both people must be into it.
• Respect boundaries—if someone says "no" or seems uncomfortable, leave them alone.
• No creeping in DMs or sending unwanted messages.
🤬 3. Swearing is Allowed
• Swearing is totally fine here! Feel free to curse casually in chat, jokes, or while gaming.
• Keep it light and friendly so everyone feels comfortable.
🚫 4. Mature Chat Allowed / NO NSFW Media
• Mature topics and conversations are fine to talk about in chat.
• NO NSFW links, photos, videos, sexual artwork, or explicit content allowed. Keep all shared media safe for work (SFW).
🕊️ 5. Leave Solved Arguments in the Past
• Please do not bring old arguments or drama back into chat.
🤖 6. Use Bot Commands in the Right Channel
• Keep all bot commands, game bots, and music bots in designated bot channels.
• Do not spam bot commands in general chat.
🛡️ 7. No Spam or Self-Promotion
• Don't spam messages, emojis, or random server pings.
• Advertising is only allowed if staff gives permission or inside promo channels.
